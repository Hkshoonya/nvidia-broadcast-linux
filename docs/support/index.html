Start with the guided setup

Open NV Broadcast and choose Help → Getting Started to replay the guided walkthrough. It explains camera setup, backgrounds, saved setups, meeting tools and power controls.

Use your processed video in another app

Complete the virtual-camera setup and follow the macOS approval prompts. In a compatible meeting, browser or streaming app, select NV Broadcast Camera as the video source. Camera permission and supported sources depend on that app.

Restore Pro access

In NV Broadcast settings, choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Manage or cancel the plan in Apple's subscription settings. Trial eligibility and the price applicable to your account appear before purchase.

iPhone and iPad

NV Broadcast mobile apps are planned for later and are not included as available features of the first Mac release. Apple's Continuity Camera can be used as an input on supported devices.

Problem reporting

Automatic in-app public reports are being prepared. Until enabled, use private email support. Your emails are not automatically published to a public tracker.
